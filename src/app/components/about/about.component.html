About Me

My Journey

I am Raviselva T, a passionate BIM Software Developer from Tamil Nadu with strong interest in building modern engineering and software solutions. I specialize in technologies like C#, .NET, WPF, MVVM, XAML, Angular, and SQL Server, and I enjoy creating clean, scalable, and user-friendly applications.

At RGS Construction Technology I build the Viskar suite of Windows applications: ViskarSteel Light, ViskarBIM, and the BIM supporting products DigiLenz and GreenPdf, powered by Viskar. I have also worked on a 3D render engine built on the VTK toolkit, and I am currently focused on software performance, using GPU rendering and multithreading to make our applications faster.

I also bring strong database skills: schema design and normalisation, SQL queries, indexes, views, stored procedures, triggers and user-defined functions. I enjoy turning new business requirements into practical technical solutions, and I keep learning new technologies.
